Heart and Soul Nebula

The Heart and Soul Nebula are two large emission nebulae located in the constellation Cassiopeia. They are massive regions of gas and dust where new stars are being formed. Their glowing shapes come from intense radiation emitted by young, hot stars inside them.

The Heart Nebula (IC 1805) and the Soul Nebula (IC 1848) are both part of a larger star-forming complex about 6,000 light-years from Earth. These nebulae are shaped by stellar winds and radiation from nearby massive stars.

These regions are important for understanding how stars are born and how interstellar gas evolves over time in our galaxy.
