Westerlund 2 Star Cluster

Westerlund 2 is a young and massive star cluster located in the constellation Carina. It is surrounded by a glowing nebula of gas and dust where thousands of stars are being born. The region appears bright and colorful because of intense radiation from very hot, massive stars.

Strong stellar winds and ultraviolet radiation from the largest stars are shaping the surrounding clouds, carving out pillars, ridges, and deep cavities in the nebula. These forces also trigger new star formation within the gas clouds.

The cluster is only a few million years old, making it extremely young in cosmic terms. It contains some of the hottest and most luminous stars in our galaxy, making it an important region for studying how stars are formed and evolve.
