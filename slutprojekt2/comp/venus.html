Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is often called Earth’s “sister planet” because of its similar size and structure. Thick clouds of carbon dioxide trap heat and make Venus the hottest planet in the solar system.

Venus has no moons and rotates very slowly compared to other planets. The planet completes one orbit around the Sun in 225 Earth days and spins in the opposite direction of most planets.

Its surface is covered with volcanoes, lava plains, and rocky mountains hidden beneath dense toxic clouds. The extreme heat and pressure make Venus a very hostile world.
