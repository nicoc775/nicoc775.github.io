Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and is best known for its bright rings made of ice, dust, and rock particles. It is a massive gas giant composed mainly of hydrogen and helium.

Saturn has over 100 known moons, including Titan, which is larger than the planet Mercury. The planet completes one orbit around the Sun in about 29 Earth years.

Although Saturn is one of the largest planets, it is very light for its size and has a low density. Its spectacular ring system makes it one of the most recognizable planets.
