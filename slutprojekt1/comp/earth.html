Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet that supports life. About 71% of its surface is covered by water, giving it the nickname “The Blue Planet”. Its atmosphere protects living organisms and helps maintain temperatures suitable for life.

Earth has one natural satellite — the Moon — which affects the tides and stabilizes the planet’ s rotation. The planet completes one orbit around the Sun in 365 days and rotates once every 24 hours.

With vast oceans, continents, mountains, forests, and deserts, Earth is home to millions of species and an incredible diversity of ecosystems.
