Crab Nebula

The Crab Nebula is a supernova remnant located in the constellation Taurus. It was formed when a massive star exploded in a supernova, and the expanding cloud of gas and dust continues to spread through space today.

This nebula is one of the most studied objects in astronomy because it contains a pulsar at its center — a rapidly spinning neutron star that emits beams of radiation like a lighthouse.

The Crab Nebula is visible in multiple wavelengths, including X-ray, optical, and infrared light, which reveals different layers and structures of the explosion’s aftermath.
