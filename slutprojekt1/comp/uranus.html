Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and is known for its pale blue color caused by methane gas in its atmosphere. It is an ice giant with extremely cold temperatures.

Uranus has 27 known moons and a faint ring system surrounding the planet. It completes one orbit around the Sun in 84 Earth years.

Unlike most planets, Uranus rotates on its side, causing unusual seasons that can last for decades. Its strange tilt makes it one of the most unique planets in the solar system.
