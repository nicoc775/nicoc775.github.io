Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest planet in the solar system. This giant gas planet is famous for the Great Red Spot, a massive storm larger than Earth that has existed for hundreds of years.

Jupiter has many moons, including Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto. The planet completes one orbit around the Sun in nearly 12 Earth years.

Made mostly of hydrogen and helium, Jupiter has a strong magnetic field and faint rings surrounding it. Its enormous gravity influences many objects across the solar system.
