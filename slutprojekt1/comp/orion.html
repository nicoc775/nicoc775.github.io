Orion Nebula

The Orion Nebula is one of the brightest and most well-known nebulae in the night sky. It is located in the constellation Orion and is a massive region of gas and dust where new stars are actively forming. Its glowing clouds are illuminated by young, hot stars.

This nebula is part of a much larger molecular cloud complex and is one of the closest star-forming regions to Earth, making it a key object for studying how stars and planetary systems are born.

Inside the nebula, powerful radiation from newborn stars shapes the surrounding gas, creating pillars, waves, and bright glowing regions that constantly evolve over time.
