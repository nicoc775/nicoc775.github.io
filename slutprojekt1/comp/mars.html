Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often called “The Red Planet” because of the iron oxide covering its surface. Its dusty landscape contains giant volcanoes, deep valleys, and rocky deserts that make it one of the most explored planets.

Mars has two small moons — Phobos and Deimos — which orbit the planet at close distances. The planet completes one orbit around the Sun in 687 Earth days and has seasons similar to Earth.

Scientists believe Mars once had liquid water on its surface, and today it remains one of the most important planets in the search for ancient microbial life and future human exploration.
