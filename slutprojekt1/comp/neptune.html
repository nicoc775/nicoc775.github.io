Neptune

Neptune is the eighth and farthest known planet from the Sun. Its deep blue appearance comes from methane in the atmosphere, and the planet is famous for its powerful storms and strong winds.

Neptune has 14 known moons, with Triton being the largest. The planet completes one orbit around the Sun in 165 Earth years.

Neptune is an ice giant with a cold and dynamic atmosphere. It was discovered through mathematical calculations before astronomers were able to observe it directly.
